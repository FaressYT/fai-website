Who we are

A national specialized company focused on designing and executing furniture and decorations of all kinds.

The factory is equipped with the latest European machines and technologies for wood manufacturing in the industrial city of Adra, using the best materials.

Our family at FAI Company aims to employ their professional experience and scientific and artistic skills to provide the best services in design and execution to elevate a national product that competes in quality and beauty with all countries that seek growth and development through work, art, and beauty.
