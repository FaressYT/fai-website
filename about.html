Who we are

A national specialized company focused on designing and executing furniture and decorations of all kinds.

The factory is equipped with the latest European machines and technologies for wood manufacturing in the industrial city of Adra, using the best materials.

Our family at FAI Company aims to employ their professional experience and scientific and artistic skills to provide the best services in design and execution to elevate a national product that competes in quality and beauty with all countries that seek growth and development through work, art, and beauty.

Goals

  • Transforming client's designs and ideas into tangible reality with high quality using materials that achieve aesthetics, durability, and sustainable design for the future within the agreed timeframe.
  • Maintaining constant communication with our clients through after-sale services.
  • Providing unique designs for furniture and decorations that combine elegance and durability at competitive prices.
  • Executing unique designs requested by clients.
  • We give customers the opportunity to choose between our unique and modern designs that meet customer's needs and tastes.
  • We are proud to be part of the reconstruction process with national hands.
  • Adopting the appropriate style for recycling wood to use more environmentally friendly wood.

Factory Overview

The factory is distinguished by its artistic vision and high craftsmanship skills where the production team works on transforming wood into exquisite artistic pieces, using their skills in designing, cutting, and finishing, in addition to relying on high technology of the machines and equipment available within the factory.

  • Developing production skills by continuously training employees on new methods and technologies.
  • Promoting innovation and creativity to produce unique pieces of high quality.
  • We strive to achieve the highest levels of quality and precision in our work, as our work involves monitoring all stages of design from planning to execution, delivery, and after-sales service.
